Python & Forecasting Projects

Medicare Enrollment Seasonal Forecasting

Built a seasonal forecasting model on thirteen years of CMS Medicare enrollment data, then tested whether the pattern was still stable before projecting it. It wasn't. January plan switching has collapsed 94% since 2021, and a naive index would have overstated the coming January by nine times.

Olist Seller Performance & SLA Analytics

Line-level OTIF analysis across 2,970 marketplace sellers, applying supply chain vendor management methodology to public e-commerce data. Built a WAR (wins above replacement) framework to rank vendors by the SLA impact of removing them, surfacing risk that a late rate alone would miss.
